Who we are

Word Dungeon is made by Jeremy Lai, an independent developer. In this policy, "we" and "us" mean Jeremy Lai.

The short version

Word Dungeon has no accounts and no servers of our own. Your progress, stats, settings and achievements are saved only on your iPhone. We, the developer, never receive them. Three outside services are built in: Google AdMob shows optional rewarded ads, Apple and RevenueCat handle purchases, and Apple Game Center is there if you choose to sign in. This page explains what each one receives.

What stays on your iPhone

Your game progress, unlocked dungeons, scores, stars, Embers, Forge upgrades, achievements, settings and any run in progress are stored on your device. They are not sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes them. Daily reminders are local notifications that your iPhone schedules itself. Sharing a result card uses the iOS share sheet, and only goes where you send it.

Third-party services

Ads (Google AdMob)

Word Dungeon's only ads are optional rewarded videos: one to revive after a defeat and one to open a sealed upgrade card. You choose when to watch one. There are no banner, pop-up or app-open ads. Word Dungeon Pro removes the need to watch them.

To show and measure these ads, Google's ad software may collect:

  • Device identifiers: your device's advertising identifier, only if you allow tracking, plus other app- or developer-scoped identifiers.
  • Approximate location: an estimate of your general area, worked out from your IP address. We never request your precise location.
  • Ad and app interactions: which ads you saw and how you interacted with them (such as video views and taps), and app launches.
  • Diagnostics: crash logs and performance data, such as launch time.

Google uses this data for advertising and analytics, under Google's privacy policy and its page on how Google uses data from apps that use its services.

Tracking and your choice (App Tracking Transparency)

After your first dungeon attempt, iOS asks whether Word Dungeon may track you. If you allow it, AdMob may use your advertising identifier, together with the data above, to show ads that fit your interests, including across other companies' apps and websites. If you choose Ask App Not to Track, the advertising identifier is not shared and you get non-personalized ads. The game works the same either way. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

If you're in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, Google's consent form asks for your choice before any ads load. You can change it later from Privacy choices in Word Dungeon's Settings.

Purchases (Apple and RevenueCat)

Word Dungeon Pro is sold through the App Store. Apple processes the payment, and we never see your card details, name or Apple ID. We use RevenueCat to confirm your purchase and unlock Pro. RevenueCat receives your purchase history (which plan, when, and its renewal status) with a random anonymous ID created on your device. That ID isn't tied to your name, email or any account. RevenueCat uses this data to provide the app's Pro features and for purchase analytics, under RevenueCat's privacy policy. Restore Purchases uses your Apple ID's purchase record to restore Pro on a new device.

To manage or cancel a subscription, go to iOS Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions.

Game Center (Apple, optional)

If you sign in to Game Center, Word Dungeon sends your leaderboard scores and achievement progress to Apple through Apple's Game Center service, so you can compare them with other players. Apple handles this data under Apple's privacy policy. We never receive your Game Center player ID, name or friends list. If you don't sign in, the game works fully and your achievements stay on your iPhone.

What we don't do

We don't sell your data. We have no account system and no analytics of our own. We don't collect your name, email, contacts, photos or precise location, and we have no servers that store anything about you.

Children

Word Dungeon isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them.
